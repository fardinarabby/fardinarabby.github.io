Undergraduate thesis · Featured work

Numerical Analysis of Passive and Active Cooling Strategies with Rooftop Solar Integration for Indoor Comfort and Energy Efficiency in Community Clinics in Bangladesh

Rural community clinics run hot, run long hours, and run on an unreliable grid. I modelled a real clinic in Khulna in DesignBuilder and tested a false ceiling, ventilated air gap and rooftop PV against each other. The optimised combination cut mean indoor temperature by 4.68 °C and annual energy consumption by 35.01%.
